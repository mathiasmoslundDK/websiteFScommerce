Common questions

Things people actually ask.

Can't find your question? Give Steven a ring — he'll know.

How do you price a job? +
We come and look at the job in person — no pressure, no obligation. You'll get a fixed-price written quote back within 24 hours, GST included, with paint & prep itemised. The price you sign is the price you pay.
What paint brands do you use? +
We're a Resene Eco-Decorator by default, and we also use Dulux and Wattyl when the spec calls for it. Happy to match whatever your designer or architect has specified.
Are you insured? +
Yes — $2M public liability cover, full Health & Safety system, Sitewise green for commercial work, and every painter on the team is qualified and inducted. NZBN registered.
How soon can you start? +
Currently booking 2–3 weeks out for residential, sooner for small jobs and emergencies. We hold a few slots each week for last-minute work — give us a ring.
Do you do prep work? +
Of course — and properly. Filling, sanding, masking, priming and any minor gib repairs are all included in the quote. A nice paint job is 80% prep and we don't shortcut it.
What areas do you cover? +
Auckland-wide, from Whangaparāoa to Pukekohe. We've also got a travelling team for Nelson and Wellington — call us if you're outside Auckland and we'll work it out.
Do you do small jobs too? +
Absolutely. Patch repairs, single rooms, fence touch-ups — through to full exterior repaints and large commercial fitouts. No job too small if we can fit it in.
What's your guarantee? +
12-month workmanship guarantee. If anything fails, peels or doesn't meet the standard, we come back and fix it — free. That's the whole "Be Nice, Do Nice Paint Work" thing.
